# Factory starting points

The factory selections are safe starting points, not promises of a finished mix. Vocal level, mic, room, performance and instrumental arrangement still determine the final settings.

- **LEAD VOCAL / NYXV** — moderate optical leveling, light EQ shaping, de-essing, a subtle air lift, controlled FET compression and restrained ambience.
- **R&B AIR** — softer compression timing and more upper harmonic presence with a little more plate-style ambience.
- **TRAP FORWARD** — firmer dynamics, upper-mid focus, short space and ping-pong delay.
- **INTIMATE** — restrained top-end enhancement, gentle compression and nearly dry ambience.
- **AD-LIB SPACE** — more reverb / delay return and a wider echo character for secondary vocal layers.

All module values remain editable after loading a starting point. Save a track preset from Ableton to keep your own preferred chain and parameter values.
